# Rice & Egg Shop Management System

## Project Overview

A Salesforce CRM application designed to manage the daily operations of a rice and egg shop, including products, sales, inventory, wholesale orders, and customer credit.

## Business Problem

The shop currently records purchases manually and has limited visibility into daily sales, available stock, wholesale orders, and outstanding customer payments.

## Project Objectives

- Maintain a product catalog for rice, eggs, and other shop items.
- Record walk-in retail sales quickly.
- Manage wholesale customer orders.
- Track inventory and stock movements.
- Record customer payments and outstanding credit.
- Create reports and dashboards for daily business performance.
